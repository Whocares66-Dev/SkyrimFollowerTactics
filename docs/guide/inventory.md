# Inventory

The `Inventory` tab contains information about what the player or follower is carrying, such as [weapons](#weapons) and [potions](#potions).

A trained follower can [learn a spell from a tome]({{ '/training/' | relative_url }}#teach-and-forget-spells) they carry.

![Inventory all]({{ "/assets/img/panel/inventory_all.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

## Weapons

The `Weapons` chip lists weapons.

![Inventory weapons]({{ "/assets/img/panel/inventory_weapons.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

`Left` and `Right` columns show [equip state]({{ '/equip-states/' | relative_url }}) in the left and right hands. Click a cell to cycle between states, such as [pinned]({{ '/equip-states/' | relative_url }}#pinned) and [banned]({{ '/equip-states/' | relative_url }}#banned).

Clicking on a weapon name shows its details.

![Weapon details]({{ "/assets/img/panel/weapon_details.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

Effect details, including conditions, are available, so you can see exactly what the weapon does:

![Weapon effects]({{ "/assets/img/panel/weapon_effects.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

### Dual-wielding

If a follower cannot [dual-wield]({{ '/combat-style/' | relative_url }}#dual-wielding), putting a weapon into their left hand will remove the one in their right hand, and vice versa.

## Arrows

The `Arrows` chip lists arrows. Arrows are separated from weapons for convenience, as they are not equipped in hands.

![Inventory arrows]({{ "/assets/img/panel/inventory_arrows.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

Clicking on an arrow name shows its details.

![Arrow details]({{ "/assets/img/panel/arrow_details.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}
