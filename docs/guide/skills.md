# Skills

The `Skills` tab shows skills and perks.

For a trained follower, click a skill to [reassign its levels and perks]({{ '/training/' | relative_url }}#skills-and-perks) on its perk tree.

![Skills]({{ "/assets/img/panel/skills.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

Custom skills added by [Custom Skills Framework](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/41780) are supported.

![Skills custom]({{ "/assets/img/panel/skills_custom.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

## Perks

Perks for which conditions are not met are shown as inactive.

![Perk inactive]({{ "/assets/img/panel/perk_inactive.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

Clicking on a perk shows its details.

![Perk details]({{ "/assets/img/panel/perk_details.png" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}
