# Effects

The `Effects` tab lists effects on the character. Click the button beside `Filter` to switch between:

- **Active** (default): effects currently acting on the character.
- **All**: also includes inactive effects and effects that make no change to the character, shown greyed out.

The button names the current view. Effects hidden by the game's own list can appear in either view; their details say whether they are hidden.
